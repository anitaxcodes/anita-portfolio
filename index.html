About Me

Curious learner, creative thinker

AD
📍 Nepal 🎓 Student 🔒 Cybersecurity

Hi! I'm Anita Dangol, a passionate beginner in cybersecurity from Nepal. I'm currently diving deep into ethical hacking, cryptography, and Linux fundamentals — building my skills one challenge at a time.

I'm particularly drawn to CTF challenges, where cryptography, web security, and logical thinking come together in creative ways. Every solved challenge feels like a small victory in my learning journey.

Alongside my cybersecurity studies, I work as an SEO content writer and digital branding specialist — blending technical precision with creative storytelling. I believe the best digital experiences live at the intersection of security, creativity, and clarity.

I'm focused on consistency, growth, and building a strong technical foundation while staying curious and creative throughout the process.
